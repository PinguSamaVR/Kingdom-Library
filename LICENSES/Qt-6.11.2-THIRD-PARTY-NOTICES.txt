QT 6.11.2 - DISTRIBUTED MODULES AND INCORPORATED THIRD-PARTY CODE

Kingdom distributes selected dynamically loaded Qt 6.11.2 libraries and
plugins from Qt Core, Qt GUI, Qt Widgets, Qt SVG, and Qt Image Formats. These
Qt modules are offered under LGPL version 3, GPL alternatives, and commercial
licenses. Kingdom relies on the LGPL option. See LGPL-3.0.txt.

The following release-specific map is derived from Qt's official 6.11.2
third-party inventory. Common license texts are included in this directory.
