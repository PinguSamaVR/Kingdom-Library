Paths beginning with sources/ and recombination/ refer to the outer bundle.
The following kit/ paths become available after extracting the kit ZIP into
its own recipient workspace, not directly inside the outer bundle.

After extracting this inner ZIP, read kit/README.txt and
kit/documents/BUILDING-AND-RELINKING.txt. The kit describes recombination
of the PySide6, PySide6.support, PySide6.support.deprecated and shiboken6
Python components frozen in Kingdom.exe, separately from external DLL/PYD
replacement. Their source is in the same pyside-setup 6.11.2 upstream
archive supplied by the source ZIP.

The kit includes binding.json, source-preparation configuration, tooling,
licenses, notices and the exact V7 text at
kit/documents/RECOMBINATION-PERMISSIONS-ADDENDUM.txt.
Only kit/recombine.py and kit/recombine_release.py are MIT licensed;
see kit/LICENSE-COVERAGE.json and kit/LICENSE-MIT.txt. This does not
relicense Kingdom, the addendum or third-party components.
